Course description

Classically, there are two main branches of the scientific discipline of demography: technical (formal) demography/demographic analysis and social demography/population studies. Technical demography focuses on collecting demographic data and analyzing it with demographic techniques (creating, examining, and projecting demographic indicators), while population studies looks at demographic processes in relation to social, economic, political, cultural, and biological factors.

DEM 601 is an introductory master's-level course in the Department of Demography, giving students a comprehensive grounding in technical demography. No prior background in demography is assumed. Students are introduced to demographic terminology and the main types of demographic data, then learn to calculate core demographic indicators, including life table indicators, and interpret them by comparison. Any calculation tool (spreadsheet software, R, or similar) is fine for coursework and assignments. By the end of the course, students will have a solid foundation in demographic measurement and basic models, able to analyze and interpret demographic data on their own.

Policies

Late work

Assignments are due before the start of the recitation session in which they are discussed. Late submissions lose 10% of the assignment's point value per day late, up to three days; work submitted more than three days late will not be accepted for credit, except in cases of documented medical or family emergency, arranged with the instructor in advance wherever possible.

Academic integrity

Students are expected to complete assignments, exams, and the course project independently unless group work is explicitly assigned. Submitting work copied from another student or from any uncredited source, or misrepresenting data or results, constitutes academic dishonesty and will be handled under Hacettepe University's academic integrity regulations, up to and including a failing grade for the assignment or the course.
